Privacy Policy | Fuel by BuZah

Effective October 11, 2026

Fuel by BuZah is a weekly meal prep business operated by BuZah Ventures LLC, a Texas limited liability company ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information we collect when you order from us or interact with our Instagram account (@buzahtheleo), how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Ordering: contact and order details you submit through our order page, such as your name, phone number, delivery details, and meal selections.
  • Instagram interactions: when you comment on our posts or message us, we receive your Instagram username, your comment or message text, and related identifiers (such as the comment ID and post ID).
  • Surveys: any answers you choose to give when we ask about your eating habits and food preferences.

How we use it

  • To take, prepare, and deliver your orders and to contact you about them.
  • To reply to your comments and messages, including sending you our order link when you ask for it.
  • To understand what our customers need so we can improve our meals and service.

Some replies to Instagram comments are drafted with the help of automated tools, including an AI model. Comments that the automation cannot confidently handle are held for the owner to review personally.

Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run the business, and only as needed:

  • Meta (Instagram): to receive and reply to comments and messages through Instagram's official tools.
  • Supabase: to store order and comment records.
  • Anthropic: comment text may be sent to its AI model to help draft a reply. We do not send payment details or delivery addresses for this purpose.

We may also disclose information if required by law.

How long we keep it

We keep information for as long as we need it to run the business, fulfill orders, and meet legal and tax obligations. Instagram comment records are kept to avoid replying twice and to review flagged comments, and are deleted on request.

Your choices and data deletion

You can ask us at any time to tell you what information we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, including any Instagram comment or message records tied to your username. Email us at the address below from the contact details you used, or message @buzahtheleo on Instagram, and we will respond within 30 days. You can also remove your own comments on Instagram at any time.

Data deletion requests:
Email buzahtheleonb@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data" and your Instagram username or the phone number used for your order.

Security

We use reputable providers and access controls to protect your information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Children

Our services are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The effective date above shows the latest version.
